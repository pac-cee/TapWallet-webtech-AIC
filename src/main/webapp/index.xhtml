TapWallet

TapWallet - NFC Digital Payment & Wallet System

A customer holds a wallet, tops it up, and pays a registered merchant. Start with Users, then Wallets.

1. Users

Register a customer or a shop operator.

2. Wallets

Every payer needs one wallet with a balance.

3. Merchants

Register the shop that will receive payments.

4. Top-Ups

Add funds to a wallet before paying.

5. Payments

Send money from a wallet to a merchant, or reverse one.

NFC Cards

Link a tap-to-pay token to a wallet.

Withdrawals

Take funds back out of a wallet.
